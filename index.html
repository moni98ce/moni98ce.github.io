Profile

From Gopalganj to complex infrastructure delivery.

My career integrates technical design, applied research, public-sector infrastructure stewardship and multidisciplinary project delivery.

Born in Gopalganj, Bangladesh, I studied at S. M. Model Primary School, S. M. Model Government High School and Government Bangabandhu College before entering BUET Civil Engineering in 1999. I graduated in 2004 and began my professional career in structural design.

I later completed graduate studies at UBC and a PhD at the University of Toronto. My work has included bridges, flyovers, buildings, utility structures, communication towers, seismic assessment, rehabilitation and advanced experimental research.
