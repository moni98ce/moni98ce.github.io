Professional journey

From Gopalganj to complex infrastructure delivery.

A career integrating consulting practice, public infrastructure, advanced research and multidisciplinary engineering leadership.

Born in Gopalganj, Bangladesh, Dr. Moni studied at S. M. Model Primary School, S. M. Model Government High School and Government Bangabandhu College before entering BUET Civil Engineering in 1999. He graduated in 2004 and began his structural engineering career in Dhaka.

He later completed graduate studies at UBC and a PhD at the University of Toronto. His work spans bridges, flyovers, high-rise buildings, utility systems, telecommunication structures, seismic assessment, rehabilitation and structural dynamics research.
