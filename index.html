About

Engineering grounded in research and real-world delivery

Born in Gopalganj, Bangladesh, I began my education at S. M. Model Primary School and S. M. Model Government High School, later completing higher secondary studies at Government Bangabandhu College.

I entered the Department of Civil Engineering at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology in 1999 and graduated in 2004. My career has since spanned consulting, government, and academia, including graduate research at the University of British Columbia and the University of Toronto.

I completed my PhD in Civil and Structural Engineering at the University of Toronto in 2024. My doctoral research focused on real-time aeroelastic hybrid simulation of a base-pivoting model building in a wind tunnel.
