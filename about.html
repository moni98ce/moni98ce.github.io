Professional profile

Moniruzzaman Moni, PhD, P.Eng., is a licensed Professional Engineer with nearly two decades of experience in structural and infrastructure engineering. His work includes bridges, culverts, retaining walls, high-rise buildings, hospitals, institutional facilities, nuclear structures, utilities, foundations, and rehabilitation programs.

He currently serves as a Senior Structural Engineer with the Ministry of Transportation Ontario, supporting bridge and culvert rehabilitation, consultant procurement, technical review, OSIM-related activities, construction engineering, and multidisciplinary coordination.

His approach combines analytical rigor with practical delivery experience gained through design consulting, public works, research, field engineering, and project management in Canada and Bangladesh.

Engineering philosophy

Good structural engineering is not only about satisfying equations. It requires sound judgement, clear communication, constructability awareness, respect for lifecycle performance, and an unwavering commitment to public safety.
