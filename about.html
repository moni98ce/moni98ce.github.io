Professional Profile

I am a licensed Professional Engineer with a PhD in Civil and Structural Engineering from the University of Toronto, a MASc from the University of British Columbia, and more than 20 years of structural engineering experience.

My work connects three perspectives: the designer who understands load paths and detailing; the owner-side engineer who must make defensible lifecycle decisions; and the researcher who tests assumptions through measurement, simulation and evidence.

What distinguishes my practice

  • Owner-side leadership for provincial bridge and culvert projects.
  • Hands-on design experience in concrete, steel, prestressed and lattice structures.
  • Cross-sector capability spanning transportation, buildings, telecom, power transmission, utilities and nuclear work.
  • Research expertise in wind engineering, structural dynamics, hybrid simulation and smart materials.
