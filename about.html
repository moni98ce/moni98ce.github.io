Biography

Born in Gopalganj, Bangladesh, on October 31, 1981, Dr. Moni completed his early education in Gopalganj before joining BUET Civil Engineering in 1999 and graduating in 2004.

He began his career at Acumen Consulting House and later completed an MASc at UBC and a PhD at the University of Toronto. His work spans bridges, high-rise buildings, public infrastructure, communication structures, seismic engineering and research.
