Professional philosophy

Rigour must lead to practical decisions.

Successful structural engineering balances safety, constructability, durability, economy and long-term asset stewardship. The objective is not only to satisfy a calculation, but to produce a solution that can be built, inspected, maintained and understood by future engineers.
