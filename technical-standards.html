Technical standards and delivery framework

Codes, standards and engineering tools organized by discipline.

Standards are applied according to jurisdiction, project scope, owner requirements and the governing edition. This page presents the technical framework without reducing the work to a wall of acronyms.

Bridges Buildings Communication Towers Transmission Towers Nuclear Materials & Delivery
01
Transportation structures

Bridges

Design, evaluation, rehabilitation, inspection and construction support for bridges, culverts and related highway structures.

Primary design standards
  • CSA S6 / Canadian Highway Bridge Design Code
  • AASHTO LRFD Bridge Design Specifications
MTO framework
  • MTO Structural Manual
  • MTO Structural Rehabilitation Manual
  • MTO OSIM Manual
  • OPSS / OPSD requirements
Applied to structural review, load evaluation, rehabilitation scoping, inspection, asset management and construction-phase decisions.
02
Buildings and facilities

Buildings

Structural design and assessment of reinforced-concrete, steel, precast and composite building systems.

Canadian framework
  • NBCC
  • Ontario Building Code
  • CSA S16
  • CSA A23.3
International framework
  • ACI 318
  • ASCE 7
  • BNBC
Applied to high-rise residential, healthcare, public, institutional, industrial and specialized facilities.
03
Telecommunication structures

Communication Towers

Wind-sensitive design of self-supporting, rooftop, greenfield, monopole and guyed telecommunication structures.

North American standards
  • CSA S37
  • TIA-222G
  • EIA/TIA-222F
British standard
  • BS 8100
Applied to tower superstructures, equipment loading, foundations, rooftop supports and serviceability checks.
04
Power transmission structures

Transmission Towers

Four-legged lattice anchor and river-crossing towers under wind, conductor, earth-wire and broken-wire conditions.

Structural design
  • BS 8100
  • BNBC
  • BS 4360
Fabrication and protection
  • BS 729
  • BS 5135
Applied to member optimization, lattice detailing, load-case development and foundation reactions.
05
Critical facilities

Nuclear

Seismic qualification, support-system design and controlled engineering documentation for nuclear-related assignments.

Applicable CSA series
  • CSA N287 series
  • CSA N289 series
  • CSA N291 series
Typical applications
  • Equipment and support qualification
  • Response-spectrum analysis
  • Seismic load combinations
Specific standards and clauses are selected according to project classification, scope and owner requirements.
