Research

Applied structural research connecting experimentation, simulation, and engineering practice.

Research interests include wind engineering, hybrid simulation, structural dynamics, seismic engineering, advanced materials, and performance assessment.

PhD research

Real-time aero-elastic hybrid simulation

Doctoral research at the University of Toronto focused on real-time aero-elastic hybrid simulation of a base-pivoting model building in a wind tunnel. The work connected physical aerodynamic testing with computational structural response.

Shape-memory-alloy reinforced concrete

Graduate research at the University of British Columbia examined the performance of shape-memory-alloy reinforced-concrete frames under extreme loading.
